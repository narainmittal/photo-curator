# Local review brief

Use this checklist yourself, or adapt it for a local assistant with image-viewing capabilities. This is an optional aid; no AI service is called by Photo Curator.

1. Confirm the desired period, display dimensions, storage budget and important experiences with the owner.
2. Read the intake report and resolve unknown dates or unreadable images. Do not edit originals.
3. Inspect each photo at useful resolution. Compare near-duplicate bursts. Exact-byte duplicates are already reported.
4. Follow the curation guide. Exclude documentation and severe blur; prefer readable faces and complete people. Track unique experiences separately from quality.
5. Fill every plan record with category, five quality ratings, a short title and a concrete reason. Set explicit safe crop coordinates where needed. Keep padded portrait exceptions as C.
6. Render and inspect every actual JPEG. Check heads, hands, activity props, exposure and sharpness. Do not call resizing sharpening blur repair. Correct the plan and re-render when necessary.
7. Where independent review is desired, have a second person or reviewer inspect the actual output and resolve disagreements. The tool does not claim this happened automatically.
8. Give the owner the local review HTML. Only their explicit A/C choices enter the finalized album. Preserve the saved CSV, source hashes, batch and export together.
9. Verify the export and total size. Upload only to the destination the owner authorized. Keep cloud backup/deletion steps separate from local preparation.
